## About

Hello! I am a Navy veteran and data analyst experienced in analyzing complex datasets to uncover patterns and support decision-making. My time spent in high pressure, precision driven environments shaped how I approach data, with a focus on accuracy, structure, and accountability. Skilled in SQL, Excel, R, and Tableau, I build end-to-end projects that clean, analyze, and visualize data to produce clear, actionable insights. With a background in global health and environment, I’m especially drawn to data with real-world stakes, where a finding doesn’t just answer a question, but can drive meaningful change.
